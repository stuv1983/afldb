## How to read it

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **BLOCKED** | Cannot proceed until a named prerequisite outside the actor's control is met. |
| **HOLD** | A deliberate stop recorded by the operator; it ends only by an operator decision. |
| **DECISION** | An operator decision is required and has not been recorded. |
| **WAIT** | Waiting on time, a scheduled event, or evidence that does not exist yet. |
| **ACTIONABLE** | Nothing blocks it; it awaits execution (usually an operator-run step). |
| **DEFERRED** | Consciously postponed; a trigger or a prioritisation step is stated or implied, so it is expected to return. |
| **UNALLOCATED** | A recorded residual, gap, limitation or follow-up that nothing blocks and nothing schedules: **no issue, owner, trigger or acceptance record**. It is **not** accepted and **not** deferred; it is simply unowned. Phrases such as "no issue opened", "not this issue", "not tracked", "recorded only", "no follow-up found" and "deliberately not fixed here" describe this label, never ACCEPTED. |
| **ACCEPTED** | A limitation for which an **explicit acceptance record** exists: the source says it is *accepted* (or records a decision/acknowledgement that accepts it, or a binding design decision that makes it intended behaviour). The row **cites that record**. Nothing is owed on it unless the operator reopens it. The absence of a follow-up never makes an item ACCEPTED, and an unresolved or unread item is never moved here. |

**COMPLETE** is not a waiting label. A row labelled COMPLETE records finished, authorised work; it is kept in section 11
(*Completed history*) so its references stay valid, and it belongs to none of the five categories below. COMPLETE does not
mean the surrounding issue is closed, committed or deployed; each row says what exactly is complete.

**Five categories** (each non-COMPLETE row's label maps to one; the *Summary by category* below lists them). **Four are waiting
categories. *Accepted limitations* is not a waiting category:** nothing is owed on those rows, so they are never counted as waiting items.

| Category | Labels | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Actionable work** | ACTIONABLE, DECISION (when the operator can decide now) | Nothing external blocks it; it awaits an operator-run step or an operator decision. |
| **External blockers** | BLOCKED, HOLD, WAIT, DECISION (when gated by another row) | Cannot proceed until time, evidence, a hold's end or another row's outcome. |
| **Deferred backlog** | DEFERRED | Postponed with a trigger or a prioritisation step. |
| **Unallocated follow-ups** | UNALLOCATED | Recorded, unowned, unscheduled and **not accepted**. Open until someone allocates, schedules or accepts it. |
| **Accepted limitations** | ACCEPTED | Accepted as it is, **on a cited explicit record**. Not blocked, not waiting, no commitment. |

"Responsible" is filled only where a record names one. Every record in these sources names **the operator** (a role);
none names an individual, so none is named here. "—" means no owner is recorded.
