## 1. Interrupted commit recovery

**Failure condition.** On a lost P6 connection, R1 lists processes and immediately takes a read-only snapshot. A still-running transaction can hold the repair locks while that snapshot shows the original 669 rows. Following the original-state branch at that instant can abandon B1a and start the build before the attempt commits. R1 also prints the probe exit status without propagating it as its own verdict. [R1 checks and probe](https://github.com/stuv1983/afldb/blob/9fc1a526/docs/v2/evidence/remediation-20261114/run6r/sitting-a1/r1-state.sh#L18-L30) · [Read-only snapshot](https://github.com/stuv1983/afldb/blob/9fc1a526/docs/v2/evidence/remediation-20261114/run6r/b1a/b1a-accept-r6r.sql#L9-L25) · [Packet 6R §4.2](https://github.com/stuv1983/afldb/blob/9fc1a526/docs/v2/evidence/remediation-20261114/run6r/RELEASE-AND-REPAIR-PACKET-6R.md#L124-L135) · [A1 stop rule](https://github.com/stuv1983/afldb/blob/9fc1a526/docs/v2/evidence/remediation-20261114/run6r/sitting-a1/APPROVAL-A1.md#L24-L28)

**Expected behaviour.** Identify the original attempt by its sitting/attempt identity. Establish that the runner and its database transaction are terminal before accepting a fresh independent snapshot. Account for a runner that has launched but has yet to acquire its locks. A process listing, missing outcome line or an old MVCC snapshot alone cannot establish termination. Uncertain lifecycle, failed probe, wrong identity or mixed state stops advancement. Reconcile terminal records with the pinned original or exact repaired state. An unexpected `ALREADY-APPLIED` or contradictory evidence stops the sitting. Never retry P6 automatically.

**Focused regression.** Use two connections and a controlled pause after UPDATE and before COMMIT. Simulate a lost caller and show that recovery withholds S3 even when an ordinary reader sees the original rows. Release the writer to COMMIT and repeat with ROLLBACK. Each run must produce the correct terminal decision with one attempted write. Include an unconfirmed live attempt and a failed probe as stop cases. Model the shell outcomes on the historical R1 cases and use real PostgreSQL for the visibility race.

## 2. Pre-write baseline drift

**Failure condition.** P5 checks the P3 fingerprints. P6’s runner compares its locked capture to its own post-update capture. An edit to another season between P5 and P6 can therefore survive alongside a committed repair and only be reported by the external AFTER probe. The packet calls for the same BEFORE/AFTER fingerprints in the commit run. [P5 baseline comparison](https://github.com/stuv1983/afldb/blob/9fc1a526/docs/v2/evidence/remediation-20261114/run6r/sitting-a1/p5-b1a-dryrun.sh#L29-L41) · [Locked capture and update](https://github.com/stuv1983/afldb/blob/9fc1a526/docs/v2/evidence/remediation-20261114/run6r/b1a/b1a-repair-2026-r6r.mts#L128-L170) · [P6 post-commit diff](https://github.com/stuv1983/afldb/blob/9fc1a526/docs/v2/evidence/remediation-20261114/run6r/sitting-a1/p6-b1a-commit.sh#L26-L49) · [Packet 6R §5.3](https://github.com/stuv1983/afldb/blob/9fc1a526/docs/v2/evidence/remediation-20261114/run6r/RELEASE-AND-REPAIR-PACKET-6R.md#L208-L223)

**Expected behaviour.** Bind P6 to the same verified, sitting-specific P3 BEFORE capture used at P5. Under the existing locks and before the first UPDATE, compare identity, player set, starting status/NULL votes, coverage and input lines, H1 (other seasons), H2 (2026 excluding status), and H3 (all coverage, including its count). Refuse missing, malformed or mismatched evidence without writing. Keep the in-transaction postconditions and independent post-commit acceptance.

**Focused regression.** Capture the baseline, pass the dry run, then commit a legitimate edit to another season before P6. P6 must refuse before UPDATE, preserve the edit and leave all target rows and row versions unchanged. Parameterise drift in H2, H3 and a captured input. The clean control must commit exactly once and pass the independent exact diff.

## 3. Same-count reversal drift

**Failure condition.** The reversal guards hash player-season rows and coverage. For 2026 round votes they compare only row count, and for workflow decisions only counts by status. A vote reassignment or value change that preserves those aggregates can pass the guards even though the packet promises to preserve intervening legitimate edits. [BEFORE inputs and fingerprints](https://github.com/stuv1983/afldb/blob/9fc1a526/docs/v2/evidence/remediation-20261114/run6r/b1a/b1a-accept-r6r.sql#L27-L40) · [Reversal guard and write](https://github.com/stuv1983/afldb/blob/9fc1a526/docs/v2/evidence/remediation-20261114/run6r/b1a/b1a-reverse-r6r.sql#L39-L83) · [Packet 6R §4.3 reversal conditions](https://github.com/stuv1983/afldb/blob/9fc1a526/docs/v2/evidence/remediation-20261114/run6r/RELEASE-AND-REPAIR-PACKET-6R.md#L152-L160)

**Expected behaviour.** Capture deterministic row-content fingerprints for the relevant scoped inputs, including round-vote identities/values, decision rows and relevant authority state. Use stable key ordering and explicit NULL handling. Bind the fingerprints to the approved baseline, compare under the reversal locks before UPDATE, and refuse any discrepancy. Keep the 669-ID, NULL-vote, H1/H2/H3 and identity guards. Historical captures lacking the additional evidence cannot qualify a successor reversal.

**Focused regression.** On a disposable repaired fixture, change one round vote without changing row count, then exercise a separate fixture with a decision-row edit preserving status totals. Both dry-run and commit-mode reversal must refuse and leave the repaired rows and the legitimate edits intact. Verify successful dry-run rollback and exact restoration on an unchanged fixture under a simulated separate approval.
